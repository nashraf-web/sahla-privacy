1. Introduction

Sahla ("we", "our", "us", "the app") is a free Arabic-learning app for English speakers. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. At a Glance

  • We do not operate servers and do not collect your personal information ourselves. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, and no cloud sync.
  • Your learning progress stays on your device and never leaves it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising data as described in Section 4, governed by Google's own privacy policies.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android settings (Section 10).

3. Information We Collect Directly

We collect, store, and transmit no personal information to our own servers. Specifically:

  • No accounts: the app has no sign-up, login, email collection, or user profiles.
  • No user content: the app has no chat, forums, comments, photo uploads, or any other user-generated content.
  • Learning data stays on your device: lesson progress, XP, streaks, hearts, and settings (e.g., Darija module toggle, transliteration display, audio preferences) are saved in the app's private on-device storage. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. (If you have Android auto-backup enabled, app-private data may be included in your own Google Drive backup — that backup is between you and Google, not us.)
  • Pronunciation practice: if you use speech features, your speech is processed by your device's built-in speech-recognition service according to your device settings. We do not receive, record, or store your voice.
  • Support emails: if you email support@huebugs.com, we receive your email address and message content, and keep it only as long as needed to respond.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

Sahla is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob automatically collects the following from your device:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., your Google Advertising ID, which you can reset at any time)
  • App interaction data — app launches, ad impressions, taps on ads, and in-app events related to ad delivery and measurement
  • Device and network information — device model, operating system version, IP address, and coarse (approximate) location derived from IP address
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance data related to the ad SDK

AdMob uses this data to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance — and, where you have consented or where applicable, to personalize ads.

Ad formats in the app: banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are entirely voluntary (you choose to watch one to earn an in-app bonus); core learning features are never locked behind ads.

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Services terms. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form is shown on first launch so you can accept or manage ad personalization before any personalized ads are served.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can also manage ad preferences in Google's My Ad Center.

5. Local Notifications

The app can send streak-reminder notifications (e.g., a daily nudge to keep your learning streak). Notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — no notification content or timing is sent to us. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in Android Settings → Apps → Sahla → Notifications.

7. Children's Privacy

Sahla is an educational app rated Everyone and may be used by children with parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we collect no personal information from anyone directly (Section 3).

Because the app shows third-party ads, we configure AdMob with child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment and a general-audiences ad content rating, consistent with Google Play's Families requirements. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the app or its ads, contact us at support@huebugs.com and we will address it promptly.

8. Data Retention

  • On-device progress: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google AdMob under Google's own retention policies — we do not control or receive that data.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

9. International Data Transfers & Legal Bases (GDPR/UK GDPR)

Google (AdMob) may process advertising data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where data-protection law requires a legal basis (e.g., EU/UK GDPR), the bases relied upon are: consent (for personalized advertising in regions where consent is required, collected via Google's UMP consent form) and legitimate interests (fraud prevention, ad measurement, and basic app functionality).

10. Your Privacy Rights

Everyone:

  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization: Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • Manage Google ad preferences: My Ad Center
  • Disable notifications: app settings or Android system settings
  • Delete all on-device app data: Android Settings → Apps → Sahla → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app

California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US state privacy laws: you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information ourselves. To the extent our advertising partner's practices qualify as "sharing" under these laws, you can opt out via your Android ad settings (above) or Google's tools. Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers, deletion requests concerning app data are completed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.

EU/UK (GDPR): you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Since we store no personal data about you, these rights are exercised against Google for advertising data (Google's privacy controls) and on your device for app data.

To exercise any right, or for any privacy question, email support@huebugs.com — we respond to all requests.

11. Data Security

Your learning data is protected by Android's app sandbox (other apps cannot read it), and because we transmit nothing to our own servers, there is minimal exposure. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Third-Party Links

The app may link to external sites (such as our website). Those sites have their own privacy practices; this policy covers the app only.
